Personalised number sets

From your details to finished sets

According to the seller, you start by entering details such as the game, the draw date, your ZIP code and your budget. Lotto Champ then combines what it found in the draw history with those inputs and returns complete number sets, with main numbers and any bonus ball, ready to copy onto a ticket.

Reference pages note that the software generates different combinations each time, so you're not locked into a single fixed "system".

Three ways to use the suggestions

  1. Play them as they are. The quickest option if you just want a ready set.
  2. Blend them. Keep a favourite number or two and let Lotto Champ fill the rest.
  3. Skip the draw. If the budget isn't there this week, not playing is a perfectly good choice.
Personalised doesn't mean predicted. A suggested set has exactly the same chance of being drawn as any other set on the same ticket type.

Who it suits

Lotto Champ suits adults who already play, want a structured routine and are happy to set a fixed budget. It's not for anyone expecting guaranteed wins or hoping to make money from the lottery.
