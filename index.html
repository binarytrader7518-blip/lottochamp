What is Lotto Champ?

A data assistant for people who already play the lottery

Most players pick birthdays, lucky numbers or a quick pick. Lotto Champ gives you another option. It organises a game's draw history (how often numbers appear, recurring pairs, gaps between appearances and how the numbers spread) and uses that to suggest complete number sets.

You stay in control. Play the suggested sets as they are, mix in your own favourites, or skip a draw entirely. Lotto Champ doesn't sell tickets or place bets. It only helps you decide what to write on the slip.

Healthy play habits

Use Lotto Champ inside a sensible routine

Treat lottery tickets as entertainment, not income or investment. A good routine looks like this:

  • Set a weekly or monthly amount you can comfortably lose, and stick to it
  • Never chase losses or borrow to buy tickets
  • Buy only from authorised retailers or your official state lottery app
  • Keep records if you win; prizes are taxable income in the US
