Lottery Scams: 7 Warning Signs to Know

Fake prize and lottery scams cost people real money. Learn the warning signs the FTC highlights and how to protect yourself and your family.

Published 2026-11-26 · Lotto Champ editorial team

Wherever there's a lottery, there are scammers pretending to hand out prizes. The Federal Trade Commission publishes clear guidance on fake prize, sweepstakes and lottery scams. Here are the signs to watch for.

7 warning signs

  1. You "won" a lottery you never entered. You can't win a draw without a ticket.
  2. You must pay to collect. Real lotteries don't charge "fees", "taxes" or "processing" before paying a prize.
  3. They want gift cards, wire transfers or crypto. These payment methods are hard to trace or reverse.
  4. They ask for bank or ID details. Never share account numbers or Social Security numbers with unsolicited contacts.
  5. Pressure and secrecy. "Act now" and "don't tell anyone" are classic tactics.
  6. Foreign lottery offers. Buying tickets in foreign lotteries by phone or mail is often illegal and a common scam setup.
  7. Messages from look-alike accounts. Check the sender carefully and contact the official lottery directly.
Buy tickets only from authorised retailers or your official state lottery app. If you think you've been targeted, report it to the FTC.
